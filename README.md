# On MetaCraft - the reimplementation cost collapse

**Status:** construction v0.1 (2026-10-13). Private. Audience: principals who
buy, sell, insure or regulate professional software - not hobbyists.
**Sister repos:** `on-hyperwar` (kinetic/strategic), `on-ai-takeover`
(systemic/economic), `on-hyperart` (aesthetic). Same ladder method, same
tooling (`just lore`, declaim pipeline).

## Thesis (one sentence)

Proprietary desktop software was never defended by law or genius but by the
labor cost of reimplementation; agent harnesses on amortized compute
collapsed that cost in 2026, the law had always permitted clean-room
reimplementation, and what remains of the moat is physical, procedural and
liability-bound - not informational.

## Design constraints (normative, see CONTRIBUTING.md)

- Rule 1 Observability: every rung a dateable public fact (commit, release,
  court ruling, price list, datasheet, filing). Private pipelines excluded
  until publicly evidenced.
- Rule 2 Hazard (HARD): institutional facts + governance criteria ONLY for
  offensive-cyber, weapons and illicit-logistics domains. No mechanisms.
- Rule 3 Legal hygiene: never describe a named project's practice in terms
  it does not use about itself ("laundering"). Quote its own words.
- Style: plain declarative, dates first, Kahn-flat, ASCII prose. Treat
  software as a cost structure, not a craft mystique.

# IX. MetaCraft

**Status:** stub v0.1 2026-10-13 `[construction]`. Source: Gemini draft s.VI,
restructured per Review E.
**Revision date:** 2026-10-13.

## Thesis

MetaCraft is the foundry that makes Crafts under agent control, with
swarms growing more capable and independent.

## Beats

1. ArtCraft as early foundry (fact, Review B): parity spec, installed
   incumbent as ground truth, agent-hour budget, checkers, shared
   standards, 13 apps at about one release per app per day.
2. Autonomy levels L0-L4 (Opus opinion, Review E). ArtCraft at L1, partial L2.
3. L3, agents building their own checkers, as the step from fast to storm
   (Opus opinion).
4. The incumbent as test bench: the foundry depends on the original it
   replaces (Opus opinion; Gemini question 4).
5. Attachment points: target choice, rule approval, release signing,
   legal accountability, named and dated.
6. Ephemeral software as hypothesis, not present (Opus pushback on Gemini).

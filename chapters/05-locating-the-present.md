Shared conventions, stated by the project: clean-room, pure Rust, native
on macOS/Windows/Linux, WebAssembly except FilmCraft as of 2026-10-13,
agent-drivable (CLI, JSON channel, MCP). Dual MIT/Apache-2.0 on
PhotoCraft; CADCraft and WordCraft READMEs say the same. Early alpha.
PhotoCraft's own 2026-10-18 README: not yet a Photoshop replacement for
daily professional work.

Not in the thirteen, and not a Crafting App:

- **ArtCraft** (`storytold/artcraft`, created 2022-03-31): generative

  here, not on the clones. Evidence filter in `book.toml` excludes this
  repo on purpose.
- **ArtCraft Launcher** (`storytold/craft-launcher`): installer/updater.
  Goliath: v0.5.1, published 2026-10-18T04:01:09Z, binary at
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ArtCraft Launcher\artcraft-launcher.exe`.

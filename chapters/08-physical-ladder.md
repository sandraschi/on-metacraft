## Thesis (Opus opinion, Review E)

The Craft method leaves the screen through software that emits machine
instructions. Rungs are ordered by the cost of a physical test.

## Beats

1. Embroidery and home cutting: stitch files and cut paths; a test costs
   cents at a kitchen table. Incumbents: Wilcom, Hatch, Brother PE-Design,
   Cricut Design Space. `[construction: prices, licensing]`
2. Knitting: machine programs; a swatch is the check. Shima Seiki, Stoll.
3. Garment: pattern CAD and 3D drape (Gerber, Lectra, CLO3D); fit needs
   bodies, so the check is medium-cost.
4. Agriculture: guidance and irrigation planning; the check is a season.
   The moat is firmware and right-to-repair, not CAD.
   `[construction: FTC v. Deere 2025]`
5. Chips: tape-out as the most expensive check; links back to Review D.
6. Close: when the license is free, the hand is the scarce part again.
